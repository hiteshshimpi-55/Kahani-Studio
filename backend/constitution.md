## Real-World Context

> *Sourced from the web via Tavily*

Activism, especially in the education sector, has a long history. Students have historically been at the forefront, challenging policies and advocating for changes in institutional, state, and federal levels. High school and college students have played significant roles in shaping educational policies through protests and advocacy. Activism typically aims to create substantive change in government or industry policies and can occur at grassroots levels, where community members organize collective actions.
